### About me

I am studying Industrial Engineering, specialising in Mechanical Engineering, at ETSII-UPM. I enjoy understanding how things work and turning theory into something that actually works: a model that simulates a mechanism, a PLC that controls a production line or a program that solves a specific problem.

I still have a lot to learn, and that is exactly what motivates me. I like working in a team, listening to people with more experience and reviewing my own work critically.

-  **Currently learning:** structural analysis, engines and vehicle theory
-  **Best results in:** Design of Experiments and Regression Models, Structure and Properties of Non-Metallic Materials (Matrícula de Honor), Electromagnetism and Python Programming (Sobresaliente)
